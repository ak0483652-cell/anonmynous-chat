Community guidelines and safety

A few simple rules to keep chats decent.

Guidelines

  • Only adults 18+ may use Strangely. Age and gender are self-reported and not verified.
  • Be respectful. Abuse, harassment, threats and hate speech are not allowed.
  • Sexual content involving minors is strictly forbidden.
  • Do not send spam, scams, advertising or anything illegal.
  • If someone says no, stop. Leave the chat and move on.

Safety tips

  • Never share your phone number, address, passwords or payment details with a stranger.
  • If someone makes you uncomfortable, tap Report or End chat. When 3 different people report a user, they get a temporary ban (1 hour the first time).
  • If you block someone, you will not be matched with them again.
  • Aisha is an AI, not a real person. Do not trust her like a real human.
  • If you are struggling or having thoughts of self-harm, please talk to someone you trust. In India, Tele-MANAS (14416) is free and open 24x7.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Strangely collects as little as possible. Here is what happens to your data.

Chat messages

Public room, one-to-one and AI messages are kept in the server’s memory only while needed to run the chat. Public room history is cleared every 3 minutes. We do not save chat messages to a database.

AI chat

Messages you send to Aisha are passed to a third-party AI service to generate a reply. Do not share personal or sensitive information in AI chats.

Sign-in and account data

If you sign in with Gmail (via Google and Firebase), we receive a unique account ID and confirm that the address is a Gmail address. Your email is never shown to other users. Likes, comments, credits, referral codes and credit activity are stored against that account ID.

Technical data

Your IP address is used temporarily to prevent abuse: blocking, reports and bans. A bot check (Cloudflare Turnstile) runs for anonymous entry. Reel videos are hosted with a media provider.
